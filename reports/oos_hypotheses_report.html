Out-of-Sample Order-Flow Hypotheses

design = 2025 · test = 2026 · 58 crypto perps (51 cross-market) · 1h bars · generated 2026-10-10

Headline

NO EDGE (H0)  All four order-flow hypotheses were re-tested on a genuine temporal split and all four fail. Every nominated effect is indistinguishable from zero — or inverts — out of sample.

This is the decisive comparison: the earlier runs used a short 2026-only design window, which manufactured small positive design-ICs. Replacing that window with a real 2025 design collapses every one of them to ~0 (or negative). The signals were in-sample artifacts.

Method (unchanged from the frozen H-series)

  • Signals at the close of bar t; outcomes from t+1 (1-bar delay); prior-range levels exclude the current bar.
  • Pooled rank IC of signal vs oriented forward return, with block-bootstrap CIs; matched controls; control regression; chronological split.
  • Universe: 58 crypto perps with 2025+2026 history (51 also spot, for divergence). 1h bars from 1m. Cost bar 12 bps.
  • Split: design = 2025-01-01 → 2026-01-01, test = 2026-01-01 → 2026-10-01.

Synthesis

  • Four for four fail out-of-sample. No hypothesis clears its preregistered bar on the 2026 holdout.
  • The design window was the whole story. With a real 2025 design, the two “promising” signals (divergence +0.0248, sweeps +0.0495) collapse to +0.0026 and −0.0024. The edge was an artifact of a short, in-sample design slice — precisely the failure mode the earlier single-regime caveat warned about.
  • Where anything appears, it is often inverted: H1's test IC is negative in both windows, and H3's incremental coefficient is significantly negative. Not zero-with-noise — actively wrong-signed.
  • No horizon rescues them. Across 1h/4h/24h/3d, H1 and H3 are negative everywhere; H2 flips sign by horizon.

Interpretation. At 1-minute→1-hour resolution, net taker flow (CVD) did not carry incremental predictive information for forward returns in 2025–2026. The proxy nature of CVD and the two-regime sample remain the standing limitations — but this is now a temporal holdout, not an in-sample fit.

Honest caveat

2025 vs 2026 is two regimes, not many. This is a far stronger test than the single-regime runs, but it is not a universal claim. The binding constraint remains regime breadth; more years (2023–2024, a real bear/high-vol stretch) and market-factor neutralisation would strengthen any future conclusion.
