# Bollinger Basis (SMA20) vs EMA Crossovers — 1h Crypto Perps

- **Universe (10):** BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, ZECUSDT, NEARUSDT, XRPUSDT, OGNUSDT, HYPEUSDT, BNBUSDT, DOGEUSDT
- **Data:** Binance USD-M perpetuals, 1h resampled from 1m; 2025-01-01 → 2026-10-01 (IS/OOS split 2026-01-01).
- **Signal:** EMA(n) crosses fixed **SMA(20)** (Bollinger middle); bands = SMA ± 2σ, σ = 20-bar **sample** std (ddof=1).
- **Execution:** signal at close *t*, fill at open *t+1*; **6.0 bps/side**; funding=on; annualisation 8760 bars/yr.
- **EMA periods:** 5, 8, 10, 12, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 75, 100, 150, 200.

## Executive summary

Across 14 EMA periods × 3 sides, the best out-of-sample Sharpe was **1.38** (long, EMA(12), OOS return 36.78%). At least one side was OOS-positive, but long-only (best Sharpe 1.38) outperformed short-only (best Sharpe -0.47). The OOS window is a single regime, so these are not independent evidence of an edge.

The pre-registered reference **EMA20/SMA20 long-short** delivered OOS return **-11.05%** at Sharpe **-0.14** (IS Sharpe -0.39), break-even ≈ **4.6 bps/side**; bootstrap 95% CI on its OOS Sharpe includes zero ([-2.50, 2.21]).

Crucially, **walk-forward** selection (choose the EMA period on each train window, test on the next unseen window) is negative on every side — the best is `long` at Sharpe **-0.33** (-21.88%, 25% positive folds). So the positive full-sample-OOS long-only result does not survive parameter selection, which is the outcome that matters.

See §10 for the point-by-point answers to the study's ten questions.

## 9. Limitations

- The study window (2025 → 2026-09) contains only two calendar blocks, and the OOS window (2026) is a single predominantly bullish/trending regime; OOS results are not independent evidence.
- Cost model is fee+slippage per side with next-open fills; no order-book, latency or market impact.
- Funding is applied on held notional at hourly-aligned funding stamps — a drag approximation, not a full cash-flow ledger (same as the prior study).
- The universe is today's top-10 liquids (survivorship bias); an equal-weight pooled book is assumed.
- Overlapping forward-return windows are handled with a block bootstrap, but multiple testing across 14 EMA periods × 3 sides × 6 filters × 2 modes is only qualitatively accounted for.

## 10. Answers to the study's questions

1. **Predictive information beyond unconditional?** Weak. Bullish-cross hit rates hover near the ~50% unconditional baseline, and only **1%** of (period, side, horizon) cells have a mean direction-aligned return whose 95% CI lies entirely above zero (§3).
2. **Which EMA periods?** The best OOS result was EMA(12) on the long side (Sharpe 1.38); neighbouring periods diverge materially (§8), so this is not a stable region, and it is not consistent across symbols (§8).
3. **Long, short, or both?** Long-only best OOS Sharpe 1.38 vs short-only -0.47 and long-short 0.67; long signals were the more useful of the two.
4. **EMA(20) vs SMA(20) special case?** It is the busiest equal-period construction (9644 OOS trades, grid max 9703) with OOS Sharpe -0.14; the exponential-vs-simple distinction at equal nominal period is not materially special.
5. **Does bandwidth help?** Best single bandwidth/squeeze variant is `bw_contracting` (entry on both EMA50) with Δ Sharpe **1.80** (OOS Sharpe 0.63), but the sign of Δ varies across periods and modes — no consistent improvement.
6. **Does price relative to basis help?** The `price_above_sma` filter moves results (it is partly collinear with the crossover state) but does not deliver a robust incremental edge on either side.
7. **Regime-dependent?** Yes. The reference config's OOS PnL is best in `up_highvol` (17.92%) and worst in `down_highvol` (-7.32%); no single regime is favourable across both calendar years (§7).
8. **Survives costs & funding?** 16 of 42 period/side combinations have a break-even above the 6 bps base cost (highest 14.3 bps). See the break-even grid in §6.
9. **Robust across periods, symbols, folds?** No. Only **25%** of walk-forward test folds were positive, neighbourhood OOS Sharpe spans [-1.02, 0.67], and per-symbol dispersion is large (§5, §8).
10. **Verdict?** The evidence does not establish a reliable edge — the best OOS Sharpe across the grid is 1.38 (EMA(12), long), the reference config's Sharpe CI includes zero, and results flip across folds/periods (§5, §8). Treat the EMA/SMA(20) crossover as a rejected standalone signal rather than a proven strategy.
